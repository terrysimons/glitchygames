# Zombies Ain't My Neighbors

A late-night creature-feature run-and-gun for up to four players, on keyboard or gamepads: four
kids at a sleepover put on their VR headsets, and the monsters of a cancelled horror show climb
out of the TV and into the neighborhood. Save the neighbors before the monsters get them. Every
sprite, sound and note of music is written in the engine's own formats. Design and decisions:
`plans/2026-11-14-zombies-aint-my-neighbors.md`. Its first stage plays: `uv run zombies-aint-my-neighbors`.
