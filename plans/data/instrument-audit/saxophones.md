## Rejected or corrected proposed entries
- maui xaphoon: rejected, alias of xaphoon. Brian Lee Wittman created the xaphoon in 1972; "Xaphoon" is his trademark and Maui Xaphoon is the maker. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xaphoon
- soprillo: renamed soprillo saxophone. A4-A7 was wrong; maker states sounding Ab4-Db7. https://www.eppelsheim.com/en/instruments/soprillo/
- tubax: A0-A3 was wrong; Eb tubax sounds Db1-A3 (VERIFIED). Eppelsheim also lists a Bb tubax (Ab0-E3), so I split tubax and bb tubax; the Tubax page also mentions a C size, no range found. https://www.eppelsheim.com/en/instruments/tubax-eb
- subcontrabass saxophone: C1-C4 was wrong; Bb, derived Ab0-E3. https://jayeaston.com/galleries/sax_family/subcontrabass_sax.html
- xaphoon: keep, but the family fits only loosely. It is keyless (nine tone holes), cylindrical bore, tenor-sax reed, overblows at the twelfth like a clarinet, chromatic range of two octaves, usually in C (also Bb, D, G). Proposed C4-C6 is plausible only for a C instrument and UNVERIFIED; Wikipedia says the C version sounds an octave below a soprano recorder of similar length (so roughly C4 at the bottom, memory-level inference). Better H-S placement: 422.211-ish (clarinet-like cylindrical bore) rather than 422.212. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xaphoon
- aulochrome: keep. Inventor François Louis (Belgium), first prototype 1999 (organology.net says 2001); two soprano saxophone pipes played separately or together; Wikipedia classes it 422.212-71. Proposed G3-G6 is wrong: sources say the same range as a soprano (Ab3-Eb6 per pipe, derived), low-A key reported on one blog. Modeling: two simultaneous detuned reed voices, fits the 4-source limit. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aulochrome ; https://bassic-sax.info/blog/?p=4523 ; https://emssound.net/13702/features/the-amazing-aulochrome
- Wrongly filed elsewhere? organology.net calls the aulochrome double-reed (contradicted by Wikipedia and the Belgian maker's description; ignore).
