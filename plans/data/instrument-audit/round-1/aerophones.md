## Notes

- Kazoo is H-S 242 (singing membranophone), not class 4, but the task named kazoo-likes. Mirliton-type voiced buzz does not map onto the engine's 4-source model without an input voice; approximate with a nasal buzzing sawtooth plus formant filter.
- Aliases that say "(missing)" depend on the matching missing row being added; if it is not, point the alias at the nearest existing entry named in the cell.
- Ranges and many H-S numbers marked "~" are approximate, not from fetched sources. Verify before relying on them. Catalog entries I could not find a clean H-S source for are mostly regional (koauau, nguru, nohkan, shinobue, daegeum, saenghwang, lusheng, karnay, trutruca).
- Alias mapping often groups regionally distinct tunings and mouthpieces; Balkan and Eastern European duct flutes (frula, svirel etc.) have different bore and fingering but near-identical timbre to tin whistle.
- Hard to model with at most 4 oscillator/noise sources: bullroarer (rotating amplitude modulation of noise), siren and slide whistle (continuous glide, easy for pitch but need smooth portamento), corrugaphone (discrete harmonic jumps), aulochrome (two voices), arghul/launeddas/khene/sho (chord clusters plus drones may consume all four sources), zampogna/tulum (double chanters plus drones), tromboon (reed buzz with slide), dungchen/didjeribone (very low drones with vocal-tract formants), kazoo (needs input voice), fujara (harmonic selection over a fixed fundamental).
- Not covered here (other slices): reed organ sub-types, pipe organ diaphone and mutation stops. Regal appears here only because it is a beating-reed aerophone.
- Hyperbass flute (listed in WW) is omitted: below human pitch range, near-inaudible fundamentals, little distinct from double contrabass flute.
- Not verified in depth (appeared only as name in LMI/WW): matofono, putorino, hne, bassanelli, dungchen details. Treat those rows as lower confidence.
- Ultrasonic dog whistle and pigeon whistle (WH) were skipped as not musical or not representable.
- Bug/oddity noticed in catalog: catalog entry "sarrusophone" is titled "Contrabass sarrusophone in C" while its name is generic. Also "pipe organ reed" is titled trumpet 8' (naming mismatch, harmless).
