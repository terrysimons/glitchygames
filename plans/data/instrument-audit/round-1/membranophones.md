## Notes

- Many pages gave no H-S number, pitch or timbre; those fields say "n/s" or "(est.)" / "general knowledge". Treat those as unverified; existence and region are sourced.
- Skipped as non-membranophone or uncertain: nyatiti (a lyre, wrongly listed under plucked membranophones in [LD]); boobam, moko/ngoc lu bronze drums (idiophones); chenda, thavil, urumi, parai, udukai, thimila, pung cholom, kebero, karyenda, sabar, gudugudu, igba, dammam, al-ras, pahu, gandang (named in sources but no description read; likely distinct but unverified; candidates for a second pass).
- "kazoo", "swazzle" need a new family (singing membranes) if the engine does not want them under voices. Engine fit: kazoo/swazzle/lion's roar need a voice-like or noise-modulated source (sawtooth through bandpass with fast AM buzz) and fit poorly; pitched drums with bends (talking drum, kanjira, tamborim, syndrum) need a pitch envelope per hit, doable. Mridangam/pakhavaj/tabla-style ringing harmonics need inharmonic partials (modal oscillators), up to the 4-source limit. Snared/jingled drums (bendir, pandeiro, kanjira) need noise plus metallic partials in the same voice. Ocean drum is noise-only with slow LFO. Khamak is a plucked-string/drum hybrid.
- Wikipedia only was consulted; MIMO and museum databases were not fetched.
