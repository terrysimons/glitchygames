## Rejected or corrected proposed entries
- jaw harp: Wikipedia describes it as a lamellophone with a tongue on a frame, mouth as resonator; this is a plucked idiophone, not a free-reed aerophone. Move to idiophones. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jew%27s_harp
- bawu: H-S 412.131 is wrong for a free reed; Wikipedia says range about an eleventh (on a G instrument, B to E), not G3-G5. Free reed set in bamboo tube. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bawu
- saenghwang: range given as hwangjong (Eb) to cheongnam-ryo (C), 17 pipes (16 originally), not A3-A5; octave unstated. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saenghwang
- pitch pipe: 13 pitches over one octave on chromatic type; "any reference note" is wrong for modern pipe. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitch_pipe
- khene: "A3-A6" unsupported; page says seven tones per octave (A minor scale), 12-18 pipes, range not given. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khene
- lusheng: "G3-G5" unsupported; page gives size classes by pipe height only, no pitch range. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lusheng
- sho: "A4-A6" unsupported; 17 pipes, 15 sound, aitake clusters, no note range. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8D_(instrument)
- hulusi: "C4-C6" unsupported; three pipes (melody plus two drones) in a gourd, no range stated. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hulusi
