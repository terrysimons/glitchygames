## Rejected or corrected proposed entries
- electronic bagpipe: REJECTED. An electrophone imitating the highland bagpipe (H-S 5), not a distinct pipe; also source "per ODD" has no range. Reuse the highland bagpipe voice. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_bagpipes (Non-traditional bagpipes).
- tulum: CORRECTED. Proposed "little drone" and origin "Turkey, Caucasus" conflict with source: droneless, two parallel chanters, northeast Turkey (Rize, Artvin, etc.). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulum_(bagpipe). Pontic dankiyo (one chanter two reeds) is separate; Azerbaijani tulug and Greek tulum are the same type.
- mashak: CORRECTED. Single reeds, can be played as drone or melody; many Indian instruments are Highland-style copies with one bass and two tenor drones (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mashak). Proposed range C4-C5 is unsourced.
- zukra: CORRECTED. Libya; double chanter ending in two cow horns; in east Libya played without a bag, like a double clarinet; mizwad (Tunisia) is built similarly. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zukra. Proposed "A3-A5" unsourced.
- zampogna: KEPT. Doubled chanters (soprano right, bass left), usually one alto drone, up to three, Marches tradition none; chanter lengths vary from the short surdullina to nearly 2 m (Monreale). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zampogna. Proposed "double reed" is right; "C4-C5, drones C2-C3" unsourced.
- biniou koz: KEPT. One-octave scale, sounds an octave above the bombarde, traditionally one drone. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biniou. Proposed C5-C6 unsourced (one-octave span only is consistent).
- Likewise launeddas (clarinets proposal) is bagless, so not a bagpipe.
- Bug in catalog worth noting: highland bagpipe pitch. Source says tonic A4 nominal (about 470-480 Hz, near Bb) with chanter spanning one tone below tonic to an octave above, nine notes. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Highland_bagpipe

- Absolute chanter ranges for nearly everything; MIMO and Grove were not reachable; NMS and MIMEd record pages exist (e.g. https://collections.ed.ac.uk/mimed/record/49686) but not read.
