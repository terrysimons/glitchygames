## Rejected or corrected proposed entries

- fences (jon rose): an installation or project, not an instrument. Reject (memory; not fetched).
- keyolin, kaisatsuko, celloridoo: obscure single-maker experimental devices; no source fetched. Keep out until verified (UNVERIFIED).
- organistrum: wheel-bowed (H-S -72), a medieval hurdy-gurdy ancestor; not a bow-bowed instrument. Keep in the wheel group or alias to hurdy gurdy.
- viola da spalla vs cello da spalla: same instrument class (see aliases).
- H-S numbers wrong for the proposal: erhu, khuuchir and sihu are 321.313-71 (spike tube lute), not 321.312-71; tro khmer/tro ou are 321.311-71; Wikipedia's 321.312 list gives gusle no -71 suffix. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_musical_instruments_by_Hornbostel%E2%80%93Sachs_number:_321.313 and ...321.311.
- gusle H-S 321.321-71 in the proposal does not match the Wikipedia list (321.312, no suffix). Low priority.
- ravanahatha range was blank in the proposal; still UNVERIFIED.

## Gaps you could not close

- Museum databases (MIMO, Met, Horniman) were not reached; a second pass should check bowed instruments of Africa (Ethiopia, Sahel, Madagascar, East Africa), Oceania, the Andes and the Caribbean.
- Sarangi variants (Sindhi, Gujarati, Nepalese) and Indian regional fiddles (kamaicha, banam, pena, behala, tar shehnai) are memory-sourced.
- European: kontra, latfiol and Apache fiddle appear on the Wikipedia Fiddle page but I did not fetch them; low-priority timbre duplicates of fiddle.
- Huqin entries from the Wikipedia list I did not include as separate rows (no source details, mostly regional): erquanqin, jing erhu, datong, hexian, huluhu, maguhu, jiaohu, zhuiqin, leiqin, sanhu, liuhu, dahu, xiqin, niutuiqin, aijieke, sataer, shaoqin (electric erhu), cizhonghu. Candidates if the owner wants huqin depth.
- Modeling difficulty: sympathetic strings (baryton, viola d amore, hardanger, nyckelharpa, sarangi, esraj, dilruba, kamaicha, gadulka) need resonant filter banks; tromba marina and tar shehnai need buzz/horn resonance; wheel instruments need a continuous bow-noise source; octobass fundamentals below 20 Hz are mostly overtones; bowed zithers/psalteries are ordinary bowed tone with slow glide and no vibrato.
