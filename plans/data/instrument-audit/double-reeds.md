General modeling note: all of these are double reeds; the engine has no reed physical model, so use a harmonic-rich oscillator with a band-pass reed formant. Cylindrical-bore types (guan, mey, balaban, duduk, kortholt, crumhorn, cornamuse, sarrusophone is conical) emphasise odd harmonics. Capped reeds (crumhorn, cornamuse, schreierpfeife, rauschpfeife, kortholt) cannot overblow and have no dynamic control, so use constant amplitude. Instruments with circular-breathing drones (ottu, nadaswaram) need a sustained envelope.

## Rejected or corrected proposed entries
- serpentine bassoon: REJECT. Proposed with family 'electrophones_and_oddities' H-S 5 and no range; only source is the oddmusic gallery index; I could not find any such instrument in Wikipedia or museum listings. Likely an alias for or confusion with the serpent, or a one-off. Wrong family for double reeds.
- tromboon: KEEP as novelty but low confidence; Wikipedia tromboon (not fetched) describes a trombone with bassoon reed. UNVERIFIED.
- piccolo oboe: KEEP but it is the same as the oboe musette; per https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oboe the musette is the sopranino oboe also called piccolo oboe, pitched in Eb or F above the oboe. Do not add 'oboe musette' separately.
- hne: CORRECTED. Proposed sound text says 'triple-reed'; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hne says a sextuple reed (hnegan) of toddy palm, with hne gyi (large) and hne galay (small). Range C4-C6 UNVERIFIED (page gives none).
- rauschpfeife: CORRECTED range. Proposed C4-C6 is two octaves; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rauschpfeife says modern instruments cover about a ninth (capped reed). Use about a ninth, e.g. C4-D5 for a soprano in C.
- kortholt: CORRECTED range. Proposed C3-C5 is two octaves; capped-reed, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kortholt gives no range; a ninth is likelier. Also page says Praetorius described only a bass; soprano/alto/tenor are modern.
- aulos: H-S number corrected to 422.121.2 per https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aulos (proposed '422.1 (~)'). It is a PAIR of pipes; model as two detuned voices.
- shehnai: page https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shehnai says double reed and also 'one set of quadruple reeds'; the page does not reconcile this. Range A3-A5 VERIFIED there, proposed G3-G5 is CORRECTED to A3-A5.
- nadaswaram: proposed B3-B5 is only two octaves; page says about two and a half octaves. Range from page, B3 start UNVERIFIED. Note spellings nagaswaram etc.
- oboe da caccia: range F3-C6 UNVERIFIED; Oboe page gives no range, calls it the Baroque antecedent of the cor anglais (F). F3 low is questionable since cor anglais goes to E3.
- dulcian: Bb1-G4 plausible; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulcian says the bass in F is most common and each size has about two and a half octaves. Bb1-G4 is 2.5 octaves. UNVERIFIED. Also it is a tenor-size claim wrong: 'approx tenor size' conflicts with bass range, so Bb1 is a bass dulcian note.
- bassanelli: UNVERIFIED, I found no page; likely alias of bassanello, and could be a bassoon-like dulcian relative. Kept pending a source.
